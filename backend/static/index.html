National Legal Metrology Portal Walkthrough

1. Dual-Persona Role-Based Access Control

The portal serves two distinct stakeholders under the Legal Metrology framework:

Govt Officer (LMO)

Enforce Legal Metrology Act, issue statutory seizure notices, audit Central Vault.

B2B Brand Owner

Pre-flight dieline audits, test packaging prototypes, prevent cylinder re-engraving costs.

Switch between Enforcement Officer and Brand Owner workspaces at any time using the identity switcher in the top navigation bar.

2. Automated Single-Scan Inspection Station

Upload a packaging photograph or capture via live camera. The unified pipeline automatically performs:

  • 3D Cylindrical De-warping: Unrolls curved cans and bottles for planar OCR.
  • Sub-Millimeter Scale Calibration: Calibrates physical scale via credit cards or Indian Rupee coins.
  • Physical QR Auto-Detection: Auto-scans on-pack QR codes for digital disclosure harmonization.
  • Rule 6/9/13/18 Compliance Audit: Verifies MRP, Net Qty, USP, Mfg Date, and Customer Care.

3. Interactive Multi-View & Virtual Caliper

Inspect physical declarations with interactive statutory tools:

  • Annotated vs. Original vs. Split-View: Instant visual comparison between raw and audited label.
  • Schedule II Virtual Caliper: Measures net quantity numeral heights down to 0.1 mm against statutory minimum thresholds.
  • Declarations Matrix Spotlighting: Hover over any statutory declaration row to spotlight its physical ink location.

4. B2B Industrial Pre-Print Artwork Studio

Designed for FMCG brand compliance teams and pre-press houses:

  • Drag & drop packaging dieline artworks before rotogravure cylinder engraving.
  • Verifies Principal Display Panel (PDP) dimensions and Schedule II font rules.
  • Pre-Press Risk Prevention: Identifies statutory defects before committing costly plate manufacturing runs.

5. Central Dossier Vault & Statutory Notices

Every inspection can be saved to the persistent Central SQLite Vault:

  • Full audit trail with officer ID, circle, timestamp, and geotagging.
  • Download tamper-evident official PDF reports complete with digital verification hashes.
  • Filter nationwide inspection records and review violation analytics.
